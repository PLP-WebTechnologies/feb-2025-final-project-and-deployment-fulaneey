Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a loving and nurturing environment where children can learn about God's Word and grow spiritually. We aim to teach them to live according to biblical principles, offering them guidance in the Lord's way.

Methods of Training

📖

Bible Stories

Engaging lessons from Scripture that teach God's truth in ways children can understand and apply.

🙏

Prayer & Worship

Teaching children to communicate with God through prayer and express their love through worship.

🎨

Creative Learning

Interactive activities using music, arts, and drama to reinforce biblical teachings.

❤️

Character Building

Developing Christ-like character through practical applications of Scripture.
